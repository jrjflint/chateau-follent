Find three words of your own

If you choose to taste, focus on one small sample. Smell it before tasting, then note one aroma, one texture and one impression of freshness. Everyday words are enough. Compare your notes with the label afterwards, rather than searching for a correct answer. Try a bite of your meal and notice whether the impression changes. Tasting is optional; you can smell, read or spit instead.

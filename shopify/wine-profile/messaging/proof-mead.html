Separate fruitiness from sweetness

If you choose to taste, compare small samples of a clearly labelled dry and off-dry wine from the same grape. Smell first, then notice sweetness on the palate and mouth-watering acidity. A fruity aroma does not necessarily mean a wine is sweet. Try a bite of your meal and notice whether the impression changes. Tasting is optional; you can smell, read or spit instead.

Notice the weight of a wine

If you choose to taste, compare small samples of a lighter and fuller-bodied wine. Notice how each feels in your mouth, rather than how strong its aroma seems. Write one texture word for each; neither needs to be better. Try a bite of your meal and notice whether the impression changes. Tasting is optional; you can smell, read or spit instead.
